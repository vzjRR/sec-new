# Knowledge base

Cumulative findings. **Never silently replace an entry** — supersede it with a dated one
that states what changed and why (`agents/KNOWLEDGE_ENGINEER.md`).

Every entry is tagged **FACT** (documented), **OBSERVATION** (measured here) or
**HYPOTHESIS** (untested — may not become a detection rule).

- `research/` — platform findings, documentation discrepancies, native behaviour
- `decisions/` — architecture decisions with rationale and alternatives
- `patterns/` — detection patterns that work, and their limits
- `false-positives/` — labelled false positives with replayable captures
- `incidents/` — notable investigations
